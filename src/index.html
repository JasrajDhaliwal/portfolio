About me

I'm a fourth-year Computing Science co-op student at the University of the Fraser Valley, focused on AI engineering. I like building AI products that real people use, from the backend pipeline to the interface.

I'm the technical co-founder of TurboCut AI, a prompt-based video editor with real, paying users, built on FastAPI, Celery, Postgres and Next.js with Claude and Gemini making the editing decisions. I've also built a real-time vision assistant for visually impaired users and an energy-aware delivery robot simulator. I'm looking for a Summer 2027 co-op in AI/ML or software engineering.

Projects

TurboCut AI

An AI video editor you talk to. Creators upload a video or import a Twitch, Kick or YouTube VOD; TurboCut transcribes it, makes a first cut automatically, and lets them refine it by chatting ("cut the intro, keep the argument"). Live with real, paying users. I handle technical development. Tech: FastAPI, Celery, Redis, Postgres, Next.js, ffmpeg on Modal, Cloudflare, Claude, Gemini, Stripe

VisionAssist

A desktop app that describes a live camera feed out loud for visually impaired users, flagging hazards with ALERT / HEADS UP priorities. Six modes: scene description, walking navigation, change detection, follow-up Q&A, reading text and finding objects. Speech streams sentence by sentence, with hands-free wake-word control. Tech: Python, OpenCV, Tkinter, Claude vision API, speech recognition

Autonomous Delivery Robot Simulator

A delivery robot planning routes across a procedurally generated hilly city. BFS, Dijkstra and A* built from scratch, a terrain-aware energy model with downhill regeneration, battery-safe FAST/ECO/V2X mode choice, charging-hub detours and deadline-based stop ordering. Rendered in 2D and as an interactive 3D city. Tech: Python, NumPy, matplotlib, Panda3D
